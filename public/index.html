A few things you may be wondering.

Can you manage more than one service? +

Yes. City Wide’s model brings multiple facility services together through one point of contact. We can discuss a single service or a coordinated plan covering recurring cleaning, maintenance, and special projects.

How do we get started? +

Request a quote or call our local office. We’ll discuss your facility, service priorities, and next steps, including a walkthrough when appropriate.

Can services work around our business hours? +

Scheduling is part of the planning conversation. Let us know your operating hours, access requirements, and other constraints so we can develop a suitable scope and schedule.

I’m already a client. Who should I contact? +

Contact your Facility Solutions Manager, or call our Southwest Los Angeles office at (562) 473-3136 and we’ll help you reach your local team.
